# Memo

**To:**  **From:**  **Re:**  **Decision needed by:**

**Recommendation.** What you ask for, and its cost.

**The problem.** In the sponsor's numbers, from the process map.

**What changes.** In business terms; no framework or model names.

**Value.** Floor case from measured inputs; upside to be tested; the assumption the case rests on.

**Risks and safeguards.** The two or three that matter, and how they are controlled.

**Pilot and decision.** Scope, comparison, duration, go/no-go criteria, owner.

**What we need from you.** Including any decision only the sponsor can make.
